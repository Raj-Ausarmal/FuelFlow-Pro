Help & Support

Helpful answers for managing your fuel tokens and account.

For your security, never share your password. A fuel token QR code should only be shown when you are ready for the operator to scan it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a fuel token?

Open My Tokens, enter your vehicle number, fuel type, and amount, then generate a token. Its QR code will be available there.

What do the token statuses mean?

WAITING means your token is ready to be scanned. AUTHORIZED means it has been assigned by an operator. EXPIRED indicates the refueling process has completed.

Why is my pump number not shown yet?

A pump is assigned by the operator when the QR code is scanned. Before then, your token may show Pump pending.

I cannot log in or change my password.

Check that you are using your current username. New passwords must include at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one number, and one special symbol.

Where can I see older activity?

Open the History tab to search your customer tokens and refueling records.
